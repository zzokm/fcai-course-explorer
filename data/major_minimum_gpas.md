# Minimum GPA Requirements for Majors

The following are the minimum cumulative GPA requirements for students to enroll in each major at the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence (FCAI):

* **Information Systems (IS):** 2.70 minimum GPA
* **Computer Science (CS):** 2.56 minimum GPA
* **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** 2.39 minimum GPA
* **Decision Support & Operations Research (DS):** 1.60 minimum GPA
* **Information Technology (IT):** 1.51 minimum GPA

If a student's GPA is below a major's threshold, they are generally not eligible to select that major unless policies change. Students can select any major where their GPA meets or exceeds the minimum threshold.
