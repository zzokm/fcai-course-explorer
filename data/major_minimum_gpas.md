# Minimum GPA Requirements for Majors

The following are the minimum cumulative GPA requirements for students to enroll in each major at the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence (FCAI) for the Senior 2028 batch (Class of 2028):

* **Information Systems (IS):** 2.61 minimum GPA
* **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** 2.56 minimum GPA
* **Computer Science (CS):** 2.51 minimum GPA
* **Information Technology (IT):** 1.56 minimum GPA
* **Decision Support & Operations Research (DS):** 1.49 minimum GPA

If a student's GPA is below a major's threshold, they are generally not eligible to select that major unless policies change. Students can select any major where their GPA meets or exceeds the minimum threshold.
